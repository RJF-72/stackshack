### Digital coupons page
- Walmart has **no traditional digital-coupon portal** and does not accept mobile coupons shown on a device. The substitute is **Walmart Cash manufacturer offers**, clipped under "Get Walmart Cash" in your Walmart account on Walmart.com or in the Walmart app. Sources: https://www.couponfyi.com/store/walmart/ ; https://thekrazycouponlady.com/tips/couponing/walmart-couponing-beginners-guide

### Coupon policy (stacking, limits, promos)
- **Accepted:** paper manufacturer coupons (including print-at-home) that scan, are unexpired, and match the exact item/size; BOGO manufacturer coupons (with rules). **Not accepted:** coupons shown on a phone, competitor/retailer coupons, expired coupons, UPC-A coupon barcodes, coupons on prepaid products/gift cards. **No coupon overage** — a coupon adjusts down to the item's price. **No competitor price matching** (ended 2017). Source: https://www.the-sun.com/money/16417372/walmart-coupon-policy-digital-devices-restrictions/
- **Stacking:** No traditional stacking — one manufacturer coupon per item; two manufacturer coupons cannot combine on the same product. A manufacturer coupon CAN combine with: a Rollback/clearance price (the core Walmart strategy); Walmart Cash offers on *other* items in the same transaction; and rebate apps like Ibotta/Fetch — but a paper coupon on an item usually blocks that item's Ibotta rebate, and paper coupons can't be used with Walmart Cash offers on the same item. Sources: https://www.consumeraffairs.com/news/how-to-coupon-at-walmart-without-getting-your-coupons-rejected-022526.html ; https://debtloansrelief.com/coupon-stacking-guide-beginners/
- **Limits:** 4 identical coupons per day; manager approval required for 4+ identical coupons in one transaction, any coupon $5+, more than $50 in coupons per transaction, or % off entire-bill coupons. Sources: https://www.the-sun.com/money/16417372/walmart-coupon-policy-digital-devices-restrictions/ ; https://rebatekey.com/blog/how-extreme-coupon-walmart-pro/
- **Recurring promos:** Walmart Deals events (this one Oct 5–11); Rollbacks; Walmart Cash manufacturer offers rotate weekly.

## Extreme Couponing Basics

### How stacking works
The classic extreme-couponing "stack" layers four savings types on one item: **sale/rollback price + store coupon + manufacturer coupon + rebate-app offer**. Example: buy Klondike 6-packs at Walmart's $3.78 rollback, then claim $3 Ibotta + $2 Fetch on the receipt = $1.28 each (The Krazy Coupon Lady, https://thekrazycouponlady.com/2026/10/06/klondike-ice-cream-bar-6-packs-as-low-as-1-24-at-walmart-or-kroger). Rebate amounts vary by account and can expire without notice.

Which stores allow the store-coupon + manufacturer-coupon stack (https://debtloansrelief.com/coupon-stacking-guide-beginners/):
- **Yes:** Target, CVS, Walgreens, Kroger, Publix, Dollar General, Family Dollar, H-E-B (yellow store coupons), United Supermarkets
- **No:** Walmart (manufacturer coupons only — no traditional store coupons; instead pair a mfr coupon with Rollback/clearance prices), ALDI (no coupons at all)

Rules that make or break a stack:
- **One manufacturer coupon + one store coupon per item** is the near-universal limit (Dollar General, Family Dollar, United, H-E-B). Two manufacturer coupons never stack on one item.
- **Most digital coupons in a store's app are manufacturer coupons** — at Dollar General, clipping two digitals for the same item won't double the savings; pair a digital with a *store* coupon instead. Source: https://www.consumeraffairs.com/news/how-to-coupon-at-dollar-general-and-actually-save-money-without-overthinking-it-041326.html
- **Paper coupons can block rebates:** at Walmart, using a paper coupon on an item means you won't earn that item's Walmart Cash offer, and you may lose the Ibotta rebate (the paper coupon takes precedence); a Walmart-app digital + Ibotta can coexist. Sources: https://www.consumeraffairs.com/news/how-to-coupon-at-walmart-without-getting-your-coupons-rejected-022526.html ; https://thekrazycouponlady.com/tips/couponing/walmart-couponing-beginners-guide
- **Threshold math matters:** Dollar General's $5 off $25 requires $25 *after* store coupons/instant savings but *before* manufacturer coupons — store coupons in your cart raise the subtotal you must reach. Family Dollar's $5/$25 similarly requires $25 after *all* other discounts. Sources: https://torokcouponhunter.com/2026/01/23/how-to-coupon-at-dollar-general-save-70-every-week/ ; https://www.familydollar.com/coupon-policy
- **No overage at these six stores:** if a coupon exceeds the item price, it adjusts down (Walmart, H-E-B, Dollar General, Family Dollar, United; ALDI takes no coupons).

### Where to find coupons
- **Sunday newspaper inserts** (e.g., SmartSource — coupon codes marked "SS" with insert date/expiration): buy inserts at sundaycouponinserts.com or bqsavings.com.
- **Printable coupons:** coupons.com; brand sites — pggoodeveryday.com (P&G), softsoap.com/en-us/coupons, scottbrand.com, colgate.com, ladyspeedstick.com, irishspring.com, palmolive.com, niveausa.com/highlights/coupons, clorox.com.
- **Digital clipping in store apps:** myDG Wallet (Dollar General), Family Dollar app Smart Coupons, H-E-B app / heb.com digital coupons, United forU, Walmart app Walmart Cash offers. **Clip immediately** — "Digital coupons can become unavailable at any time." (The Krazy Coupon Lady)
- **Coupon databases:** hotcouponworld.com and freestufftimes.com coupon databases for searching by brand/item.
- **Peelies and blinkies:** peel-off coupons on product packaging; blinkie dispensers hanging in aisles near related products. Source: https://www.historytools.org/consumer/does-dollar-general-take-coupons
- **Rebate and freebie sites/apps:** Ibotta, Fetch Rewards, Checkout 51, Shopkick, Swagbucks, MyPoints, Merryfield; brand rebate sites (e.g., spookiezoocash.com for Keebler).

### Common beginner mistakes
1. **Clipping two digital coupons for one item** at DG/Family Dollar — most app digitals are manufacturer coupons, so only one applies; pair with a store coupon instead. Source: consumeraffairs.com DG guide.
2. **Not clipping fast enough** — digital coupons and rebate offers can vanish anytime; clip before you shop. Source: KCL.
3. **Misbuilding threshold carts** — at Dollar General, adding store coupons to a $25 cart can drop you below the $5/$25 threshold after they're applied. Source: torokcouponhunter.com.
4. **Stacking a paper coupon with a rebate on the same item** at Walmart — the paper coupon takes precedence and you lose the Ibotta rebate or Walmart Cash. Source: KCL Walmart guides.
5. **Ignoring limits** — 4 identical coupons per day at Walmart and Family Dollar; "Limit 1 per customer" on most H-E-B digitals; limit 2 on United forU coupons. Sources: the-sun.com; familydollar.com/coupon-policy.
6. **Wrong size/variety or expired coupon** — coupons work only for the exact item, brand, and size stated. Sources: H-E-B coupon policy (dev.passionforsavings.com); the-sun.com.
7. **Using blurry, altered, or photocopied printables** — stores reject them; copying coupons is coupon fraud. Sources: robinspost.com; H-E-B policy.
8. **Paying delivery fees that erase the savings** — DoorDash/Uber Eats fees, service fees, and tips can cost more than a small dollar-store haul; many coupons and sale prices are in-store only. Sources: https://www.marketplace.org/story/2026/06/01/does-dollar-store-delivery-cancel-out-a-good-deal ; KCL (7 Days of Savings: in-store only).
9. **Not verifying in the app before checkout** — scan items in the DG/Family Dollar app to confirm coupons attach; don't assume online prices match in-store. Sources: lemon8 DG couponing tips; groupon.com DG FAQs.
